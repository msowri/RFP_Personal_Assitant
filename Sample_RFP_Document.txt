REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL
IT Infrastructure Modernization Project

RFP-2024-015
Issue Date: May 8, 2024
Due Date: June 15, 2024

1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
ABC Corporation is requesting proposals from qualified IT service providers for a comprehensive infrastructure modernization project. The project involves migrating our current on-premise infrastructure to a cloud-based solution while ensuring business continuity and data security.

Company Background:
- Fortune 500 manufacturing company
- 5,000 employees across 3 locations
- Annual revenue: $2.5 billion
- Current IT infrastructure: 15 years old

2. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

2.1 Cloud Infrastructure
- Provider must demonstrate expertise in enterprise-scale cloud deployments
- Minimum 99.9% uptime Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Support for multi-cloud environments (AWS and Azure preferred)
- Auto-scaling capabilities to handle seasonal demand fluctuations
- Disaster recovery and business continuity planning

2.2 Security and Compliance
- ISO 27001:2013 certification mandatory
- SOC 2 Type II compliance required
- End-to-end encryption for all data transmission
- Multi-factor authentication implementation
- Regular security audits and penetration testing
- GDPR compliance for European operations
- HIPAA compliance for healthcare data handling

2.3 Integration Requirements
- RESTful API development and integration
- Single Sign-On (SSO) implementation
- Integration with existing ERP system (SAP)
- Legacy system migration support (25+ systems)
- Real-time data synchronization capabilities

2.4 Performance Requirements
- Support for 10,000+ concurrent users
- Response time <200ms for critical applications
- Data processing capacity: 100TB+ monthly
- Backup and restore within 4 hours RTO/RPO

3. PROJECT SCOPE AND DELIVERABLES

3.1 Current Environment Assessment
- Comprehensive infrastructure audit
- Application dependency mapping
- Performance baseline establishment
- Risk assessment and mitigation planning

3.2 Migration Strategy
- Phased migration approach
- Minimal downtime (<4 hours per phase)
- Data integrity verification
- Rollback procedures for each phase

3.3 Implementation Deliverables
- Cloud architecture design and implementation
- Security framework deployment
- API integration for 15 critical systems
- Staff training program (20 IT personnel)
- Documentation and knowledge transfer

3.4 Post-Migration Support
- 24/7 monitoring and support
- Performance optimization
- Regular security updates
- Capacity planning and scaling

4. PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

4.1 Company Qualifications
- Minimum 15 years in IT infrastructure services
- Minimum $50M annual revenue
- At least 5 similar enterprise projects in past 3 years
- Minimum 500 employees company-wide
- Financial stability (positive cash flow for 3+ years)

4.2 Technical Approach
- Detailed migration methodology with timeline
- Risk assessment and mitigation strategies
- Quality assurance and testing procedures
- Change management approach
- Resource allocation plan

4.3 Pricing Structure
- Fixed-price model for implementation
- Optional managed services pricing
- Payment milestones aligned with deliverables
- 3-year total cost of ownership projection
- Pricing for additional services and support

4.4 Required Documentation
- Company profile and financial statements
- Technical certifications and compliance documents
- Case studies from similar projects
- Client references (minimum 3)
- Project team resumes and certifications

5. EVALUATION CRITERIA

5.1 Technical Capability (40% weight)
- Relevant experience and expertise (15%)
- Technical approach quality (10%)
- Innovation and best practices (10%)
- Risk management (5%)

5.2 Cost Competitiveness (30% weight)
- Competitive pricing (15%)
- Value for money (10%)
- Transparent pricing structure (5%)

5.3 Company Profile (20% weight)
- Financial stability (10%)
- Client references (5%)
- Certifications and compliance (5%)

5.4 Project Management (10% weight)
- Project methodology (5%)
- Resource allocation (3%)
- Timeline realism (2%)

6. KEY QUESTIONS FOR PROPOSERS

6.1 Experience and Capabilities
1. What is your company's experience with enterprise-scale cloud infrastructure modernization?
2. How do you ensure data security and compliance during migration processes?
3. Can you provide examples of similar projects completed in the last 3 years?
4. What certifications does your company currently hold?
5. How do you handle change management and user training?

6.2 Technical Approach
6. What is your typical implementation timeline for a project of this scale?
7. How do you ensure minimal downtime during critical migration phases?
8. What is your approach to legacy system integration?
9. How do you handle data integrity and validation during migration?
10. What monitoring and management tools do you provide?

6.3 Pricing and Support
11. What are your pricing models for ongoing support and maintenance?
12. Can you provide client references from similar enterprise projects?
13. What is your approach to capacity planning and future scaling?
14. How do you handle scope changes and additional requirements?
15. What are your SLA guarantees for post-migration support?
